## How To Configure Single Sign-On

Single sign-on (SSO) allows users to login once and access all applications.
This guide will explain how the administrator should configure SSO. It's simply
a matter of following the steps below, e.g. adding your identity provider,
whitelisting domains etc.

1. Click on the Settings button in the top-right corner and then click Security.
2. The Identity Provider field should be populated by the admin with the URL of the IdP.
3. Please enter 3 or more allowed domains via the Domains box.
4. Click Save on the File menu.

Once SSO has been configured, the user may be required to log in again. If he
experiences errors, he should utilize the Troubleshooting page.
